Day at a glance

Getting There

Your Route: Sorrento → Capri

Since you are based in Massa Lubrense, Sorrento (Marina Piccola) is your closest and most practical departure port — approximately 12 minutes by car. This is the correct choice over Naples, which would require driving north against the entire day's logic.

Departure port: All ferry operators depart from Marina Piccola, Sorrento. All arrive at Marina Grande, Capri. From Marina Grande, both Laser Capri (Pier 23) and Motoscafisti (Dock 0) are within a 2-minute walk of any ferry pier — no buses or transfers required.
🚗 Parking in Sorrento: Non-residents cannot bring vehicles to Capri during high season (April–October). Leave your car in Sorrento before boarding.

Garage Marina Piccola — closest to the pier, ~€40/day. City-centre parking lots with shuttle service are cheaper and worth considering for a full-day trip.
💡 Pro tip: Sit on the left side of the boat from Sorrento. You'll see the cliffs of the Sorrento Peninsula, Bagni della Regina Giovanna, Massa Lubrense village, Punta Campanella, and the best approach view of Marina Grande on arrival.

Key Facts

Blue Grotto — Facts & Late June Caveats

The basics
  • Open daily 09:00–17:00, weather and sea conditions permitting
  • Entry by small rowing boats only — no swimming during open hours
  • Each morning at 09:00, skippers evaluate whether it is safe to enter
  • Entrance is a 1-metre-high cavity — impossible in rough or high seas
  • Motoscafisti publishes live open/closed status on their homepage
Late June crowd reality
  • Cruise ships dock from ~09:30–10:00 onward — queues build fast
  • Peak queue time: 10:30–13:00
  • First boat tour (09:00–09:30) gives the best chance of short wait
  • Weekdays significantly better than weekends
  • If your wait exceeds 45 min, boat returns; ticket stays valid for next departure
⚠ Important — The €18 entrance fee: The Blue Grotto entrance fee is a state charge paid directly at the cave on the day. It is not included in any boat company's ticket — regardless of how their marketing is worded. €18 per person applies to all visitors, always.
